# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission J2 (Klingon side)

Joint mission J2 ("Absent Friends", `redwrld`'s `j2` map, the barren world
Orgolio), the fourth mission of the Klingon campaign, in a 40-minute
recording with commentary (YouTube `oHo6STxIMaE`, titled only "Klingon
Mission 4"). Identified by its audio: only the seven `J2K` clips match.
Observed behaviour; messages paraphrased. October 2026.

This table puts the two BACKGROUND DATA pages (3, 4) and INCOMING
TRANSMISSION (6) before STELLAR CARTOGRAPHY (7), and the briefing **doesn't
follow the table's order**: it opens with STELLAR CARTOGRAPHY and PLANETARY
DATA, then OBJECTIVE, and the background pages never appear; four clips
play over views with no page. So the order of steps, and which pages they
show, can't come from the string table alone: the briefing is scripted per
mission, like the rest of the mission. What holds in every mission seen
(F1, K1, K2, J1, J2): **the clips play in number order, one per step,
STELLAR CARTOGRAPHY and PLANETARY DATA first**.

The table also holds a second mission, "Head to Head" (strings 13-19,
with its own title, stardate and objectives: destroy each side's hub),
presumably the map's multiplayer variant.
