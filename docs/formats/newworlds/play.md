## What they established

- **The campaigns' order**: the Klingons' K1, K2, J1, J2, J3, K7, J4, K8,
  J6, J7, K12, J8, K14, J9, and the Federation's F1, F2, F3, J1, J2, J3,
  J4, F8, J6, J7, F12, J8, F14, J9 (its videos numbered 1-14, matched by
  their voice clips), and the Romulans' R1, R2, J1, J2, J3, J4, R8, R9, J6,
  J7, R12, J8, R14, J9 (their videos numbered 1-15 with no 7: R8 isn't
  recorded): level.dat's order, with the joint missions played as the
  side's own and no J5 (the voice folders have none either). The loading
  screen names each "Klingon level NN" ("ROMULAN LEVEL NN"), the joint
  ones "JOINT LEVEL NN", the bonus ones "BONUS ROMULAN MISSION".
- **The bonus missions** are played on other missions' maps: C11 on
  `f1` (its mines and ore readings), C10 most likely on `f2`, C9 on `f3`
  or `j3`.
- **Federation and Romulan briefings open with the transmission page**
  over the emblem, silent, before the first clip (every Federation and
  Romulan recording; of the Klingon ones only J8 and J3).
- **Briefings** play the mission's voice clips in number order, one per
  step (a lettered clip such as `K12009A` an extra step after its number),
  usually STELLAR CARTOGRAPHY and PLANETARY DATA first. Which page and view
  go with each step is per mission: pages in the table's order, some left
  out, often several clips over the emblem screen with no page. See
  [media.md](media.md).
- **Map positions** in the game (Tricorder readouts, message positions)
  are eighths of a block, row first: the 19 exhausted mines of the recorded
  missions with known maps lie on (one beside) deposits read so, and so do
  the Romulan recordings' 25 more. See [gmf.md](gmf.md#map-positions-in-the-game).
  One reading in the notes doesn't: J2F's mine at 196, 436, probably
  misread ([play-j2r.md](play-j2r.md)).
- **Deposit kinds** are the six raw ores: 1 dilithium, 2 talgonite, 3
  silicon, 4 kelbonite, 5 magnesite, 6 dolamide. The Tricorder names the
  ore under the cursor over its relief inset ([gmf.md](gmf.md#resource-deposits)).
- **Mission 14 is played on mission 12's map** (K14's mine on `k12.gmf`).
- **Messages** come in two kinds: generic ones shared by every mission and
  side (a building finished, short of power, short of resources or crew, a
  mine exhausted, all objectives met, in joint missions each rival's hub
  destroyed or mission failed) and the mission script's own (discoveries
  with positions, natives' and off-planet senders' messages, scans and
  research in steps, captures, timed endings).
- **Buildings and units** by name (the selection panel), matched to the
  models: [play-f1.md](play-f1.md#buildings-and-their-models),
  [play-k1.md](play-k1.md#buildings-and-their-models). Upgrades are pieces
  added round a building's first model, each selectable by name.
- **Briefing model views** differ by side: the Federation's draw the
  model see-through and lavender over a teal grid (F2's derelict, J4F's
  Metar portal and nak, J8F's vehicle yard), the Klingons' solid under a
  dark blue light over a red grid (K2's temple, its glow still green),
  the Romulans' see-through and blue-violet over a grid of green crosses.
  Every model a recorded briefing shows is now matched; see the mission
  pages.
- **Starting stock** (500, 1000, 2300, 500, 4000, 5000; the Romulans'
  500, 988, 2211, 497, 3500, 5000 in most missions, and much more in later
  ones: J4R, J7R, J8R, R12, R14) and colony capacity (50, or 90 in later
  missions). Capacity grows in play (R1: 50, 55, 75, 90; J4R 50, 75, 80,
  90), so K2's 90 at the start may be after growth.
- **The Communications panel moves**: it can sit at the top centre as well
  as the bottom (R9, R12, C11), presumably dragged there.
- **Crew portraits** go by sender section, and a section can have more
  than one face (the Romulans' Engineering and Intelligence each have two).
