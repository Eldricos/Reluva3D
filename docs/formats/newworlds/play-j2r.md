# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission J2 (Romulan side)

Joint mission J2 ("Absent Friends", `redwrld`'s `j2` map, the barren world
Orgolio in the Jotunheim system) played as the Romulans, in a 33.6-minute
recording (2018.5 s) with commentary (the Drive file "Romulan Mission 4").
Times are the recording's; messages are
paraphrased. Other sides: [play-j2f.md](play-j2f.md),
[play-j2k.md](play-j2k.md).

Identified three ways: the loading screen (0:05-0:12) reads "JOINT LEVEL 02"
(with progress lines for the briefing, the officers and the geographical
data, and the percent-done lines) over the Federation, Klingon and Romulan emblems; the audio holds the
seven `J2R` clips (`J2R005`-`J2R011`); and both exhausted mines lie on
deposits of `j2` (below). So the fourth Romulan mission is J2, matching the
level.dat order R1, R2, J1, J2 (the third being J1,
[play-j1r.md](play-j1r.md)).
