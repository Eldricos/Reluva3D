# The Fallen: research notes

Working notes, kept as they were written: they don't follow the page
templates, and they may hold guesses since settled or corrected. What the
engine relies on is on its pages in [formats/](../formats/) and
[systems/](../systems/), each with its source.

The notes compare Reluva with recordings of the original game. Times are
given in seconds from a level's load in the recording (where the frame
stops changing on the LOADING panel) or from the recording's start;
places in a level as Unreal units. They use no confidence marks of
their own.
