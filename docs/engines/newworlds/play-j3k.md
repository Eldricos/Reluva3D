Joint mission J3 ("The Mask Slips", `alpwrld`'s `j3` map, the windswept
planet AP173.B), the fifth Klingon mission, in a 73-minute recording with
commentary (YouTube `m2VAoJT_k0U`). Identified by its audio: only the ten
`J3K` clips match. Observed behaviour; messages paraphrased. October 2026.

with the sender's name as the heading, a **timed ending** (survive until the
ship arrives, about 16 minutes after its announcement), and a story
twist (the anti-Hubrin weapon fires at the central tower, 33:56). The
opponents are the **Metar** (the "colony core" objective, `mch` in
`forbuild.cat`) and the Taubat.

at 40:35. The mission ends in the colony at 72:30, with the loading screen
of the next level at 73:00.
