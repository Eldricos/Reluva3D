# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission R12 (Romulan)

Romulan mission R12 ("Death Rattle", `rom12.stb` string 1; the planet
Bezouk 2, `icewrld`'s `r12` map) in a 66.7-minute recording (3999.1 s) with
commentary (the Drive file "Romulan Mission 11"). Times are
the recording's; messages and pages are paraphrased. Guesses are marked.
Federation side: [play-f12.md](play-f12.md); Klingon side:
[play-k12.md](play-k12.md).

Identified three ways: the loading box reads "ROMULAN LEVEL 12" (0:05-0:10,
and on the debriefing box at 66:30); four `R12` clips (`R12006`-`R12009`)
are in the audio; and all four exhausted mines and both mineral readings lie
on kind-1 deposit blocks of `r12` (below). Campaign place (level.dat): R1,
R2, J1-J4, R8, R9, J6, J7, **R12**, J8, R14, J9 - the eleventh Romulan
mission, matching the Drive file's number (files go by campaign place).
