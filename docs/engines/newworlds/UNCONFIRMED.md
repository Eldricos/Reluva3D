# Unconfirmed items (Star Trek: New Worlds)

What Reluva does that hasn't been checked against the running game. Most of
it is listed where it belongs, in each page's open questions; this page
gathers them. [vm-checklist.md](vm-checklist.md) says what to collect in the
game to settle them.

## Guessed or by eye

- **The play layer's figures** (speeds, camera distances, build times,
  costs, power, mining, refining, crew growth): each placeholder and its
  source is in [game.md](game.md#placeholders).
- **The starting units:** four of the side's ground scouts. Every recorded
  colony starts with four vehicles of one kind; which kind isn't known
  ([game.md](game.md#whats-modelled)).
- **Construction:** how many construction bees the game sends, and whether
  they come from the construction yard; whether the game forbids building on
  deposits ([game.md](game.md#the-colony)).
- **Mining:** which ore makes which material ([game.md](game.md#the-colony),
  [gmf.md](gmf.md#open-questions)).

## Inferred, not seen

- **Mission script blocks the recordings didn't show** (a fail never
  reached, a message the panel hid) are inferred from the objective and
  fail strings, and marked "(inferred)" in the scripts
  ([mission-scripts.md](mission-scripts.md#how-far-they-go)).
- **The starting colonies' layouts** ([colony.md](colony.md)).
