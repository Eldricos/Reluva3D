# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission J4 (Klingon side)

Joint mission J4 ("Cry Havoc", `alnwrld`'s `j4` map, the bleak world
Al-Fadir), the seventh Klingon mission, in a 78-minute recording (YouTube
`TVafnsNES-g`). All eleven `J4K` clips found. Observed behaviour; messages
paraphrased. October 2026.

## Buildings and units

Selections (39) name the known buildings plus a **"vehicle yard upgrade"**;
a **MULTISELECTION** panel for several units; units: armed transport,
ground scout, disruptor battery. The Metar structures are blue-and-purple
spires and ships (`forbuild.cat`'s `mch`, `hcp`-like towers).

## Positions

Its six exhausted mines are the largest set from one map; with the others
they fixed the game's coordinates as eighths of a block, row first
([gmf.md](../formats/gmf.md#map-positions-in-the-game)).
