# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission K2

Klingon mission K2 ("The Green and the Gray", `vlcwrld`'s `k2` map, the
volcanic world Ixion) as seen in a 37½-minute recording with commentary
(YouTube `S32n4_IWLvo`; laid out like the K1 recording,
[play-k1.md](play-k1.md)). Observed behaviour; times are the recording's.
Messages are paraphrased. October 2026.

## Loading and briefing (0:00-3:10)

The loading screen names the level, "KLINGON LEVEL 02", over the three
sides' emblems, with progress lines ("initializing briefing", "loading
officer command protocols") and `frend.stb`'s "Done: %d%%".

All eight K2 clips were found in the audio (0.40-0.88 against 0.09 or
less elsewhere), back to back:

| Clip | Starts | Page (`klg02.stb` id) |

Here a clip is its page's id **+ 1**, not + 3 as in K1: the table's first
two pages (INCOMING TRANSMISSION and a RECEIVING page, strings 2 and 3)
get no clip and aren't shown. So the rule across F1, K1 and K2 is: **one
clip per briefing step, in number order, starting with STELLAR
CARTOGRAPHY**, the steps being the table's heading pages in order except
the transmission ones, plus (in F1 and K2) a last clip on the emblem
screen.

## Buildings and units

The selection panel (52 selections) names the same Klingon buildings as in
K1 (colony hub, construction yard, vehicle yard, science station, sickbay,
mining station, livestock farm, arsenal, adv. power generator), plus
**upgrade pieces with their own names**: "Science station upgrade" and
"Resource processor upg" (the abbreviation the game's). Units: photon
artillery, disruptor tank, ground scout, disruptor battery. Clicking a
vehicle yard shows an ENGINEERING panel with the Klingon emblem and the
vehicle being built. A STAFF panel again lists officers (name, section,
skill BASIC or SKILLED, post).

The rebel Taubat buildings in the last minutes are the same models as K1's
(`tt` temple, `tw` triangles, the pyramids `th`); the colony is built on
concrete as in the other missions.

## Positions

The mine positions reported here lie on deposits of the map once read as
eighths of a block, row first; see [gmf.md](../formats/gmf.md#map-positions-in-the-game),
where all the recordings' mines are put together. (An earlier reading, 16

## Colony

Colony size 35/90 at 6:20, 53/90 at 12:13, 64/90 at 17:47, 81/90 at
26:31: K2 starts with a larger capacity (90) than K1 and F1 (50).
