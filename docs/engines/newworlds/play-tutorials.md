# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, the training missions

A third-party Let's Play ("Colony Commander's first steps", 1:52:47, no
commentary transcribed) of the Federation training: the training mission C1
("Duty first.") and the five tutorials C4-C8 (`cvr03.stb`). Observed
behaviour; times are the recording's. Every message and page is
paraphrased. October 2026.

The picture is pillarboxed like the campaign recordings: a 4:3 game image
960x720 at x=160 in the 1280x720 video. The in-game resolution can't be
told from the video (panels and fonts are the same proportions as in the
640x480 recordings).

## Main menu and setup screens

- **Main menu** (0:00): the Star Trek New Worlds logo in a silver frame over
  a purple starfield, a scrolling text box (version "v1.0", Interplay and
  Paramount notices), and six buttons in two rows: **Single Player
  Campaign, Multiplayer Launch, Options** / **Introductory Movie, About,
  EXIT**. Options, About and Multiplayer are never opened.
- **Single Player Campaign** (1:09): title top right; a **Race and
  Difficulty** box with three difficulty labels (Average, Expert, Novice)
  and three side buttons (Federation, Romulan, Klingon), a **Tutorial**
  button under them (selected), a **Cadet Mode** checkbox for an easy game
  (unticked), a planet picture (a pale green-white world with a smaller
  moon/planet and captions too small to read; presumably Jagganath, string
  2), a **Select Mission** list (Tutorial, Building, Mining Resources,
  Power, Defense, Combat: level.dat's C1, C4-C8 by name) and **Back /
  Play**. No resolution or multiplayer settings are shown.
- **Briefing**: the same layout as the campaigns: a small INSTRUCTION box
  (green title bar) with the page, a view behind it, **NEXT** and
  **MISSION** buttons bottom right. Each page lasts its clip.
- **In-game quit** (48:46): a small box with a portrait asking whether to
  quit the game, YES / NO; YES goes straight to the main menu.

## Interface (Federation, purple panels)

- **Top centre**: a compass strip ("---350---+---[ N ]---+---010---"),
  which in the full Tricorder becomes the hover readout.
- **NEW WORLDS** panel (top right): the selection's name (COLONY HUB, CON
  YARD, STORAGE FACILITY / MATERIAL STORAGE (both labels seen), VEHICLE
  YARD, RESOURCE PROCESSOR, MINING STATION, POWER GENERATOR, SECURITY
  CENTER, GROUND SCOUT, ARMED TRANSPORT, MULTISELECTION, COLONY DETAILS);
  a status block (owner FEDERATION, OPERATIONAL / INCOMPLETE / READY / EN
  ROUTE, SHIELD COVER BASIC or HIGH, EFFICIENCY n%, a green health bar);
  rows with icons: power (GENERATOR level LOW/MED, CONSUMPTION, DEMAND MET,
  RESERVE; or CONSUMER, SUPPLY, RESERVE), staff (OFFICERS, CREW, POOL),
  vehicles (WORKBEE, CARGOBEE, CNSTRCTNBEE, TRANSPORT), raw ores (RAW
  DILITHIUM, TALGONITE, SILICON, KELBONITE, MAGNESITE, DOLAMIDE) or
  materials (DILITHIUM, POLYCOMP, THERMOCONC, TRANS AL, NITRIUM, DURANIUM),
  COLONY SIZE. Bottom: page arrows (the right one lights when an upgrade is
  available) and buttons that vary: **INFO / BUILD / STAFF** (hub), **RAZE
  / BUILD** (other buildings), BUILD only (storage).
  - **INFO** on the hub: COLONY SIZE a/b, the six materials, and a **JUMP
    TO** grid of building icons.
  - **COLONY DETAILS**: power totals, crew READY / ACTIVE / AVG HEALTH, raw
    ores, materials.
  - **STAFF**: officer cards (portrait, rank and name, section, skill
    BASIC / SKILLED / HIGHLY SKILLED, the building they are in) and a
    **TRAINING** button; **TRAINING** lists who can train in what (Bellah
    in operations, Dante in engineering). Officers seen: Ens.
    Bellah (Operations, basic), Lt. Dante (Engineering, highly skilled;
    moved from the hub to the resource processor in C8), Lt. Gage
    (Security, skilled).
  - **ENGINEERING** (vehicle yard BUILD): a queue ("ENGINEERING: 1 OF 1",
    UNDER CONSTRUCTION, items IN QUEUE).
- **Build menu**: the panel turns into a column of three model icons per
  page (title NEW WORLDS for structures, ENGINEERING for vehicles), the
  Federation emblem top left, arrows and X at the bottom; hovering shows
  name, a one-line role and cost.
- **ALLY** opens a page about setting standing orders towards each race
  (31:40).
- **TRICORDER** (bottom right): header line shows the mode or hover
  (TERRAIN AT <a:b>, RELIEF X2, IMPASSABLE, FRIENDLY STRUCTURE, FRIENDLY
  UNIT, and over deposits the ore: TALGONITE AT, SILICON AT, DILITHIUM AT,
  MAGNESITE ORE AT); buttons **COMMS, ALLY, ZOOM**. Normally a tilted 3D
  relief mini-map; with Tab it fills the screen and the small window shows
  the camera view instead. Own buildings are blue squares, deposits
  coloured crosshatch (yellow, green, cyan, pink, red), playbook icons
  pop up on the left with tooltips ("ATTACK EN ROUTE").
- **COMMUNICATIONS** (bottom centre): title, portrait, section name, text,
  counter n/N; **DELETE**, first/previous/next/last arrows, **MISSION**, X.
- **COMMAND** (MISSION button, 39:40): the mission name and title
  (POWER / TUTORIAL 3…), then each MISSION OBJECTIVE with INCOMPLETE or
  COMPLETE, each with the emblem.
- **Portraits**: Command and C1's Operations: a young woman, light-brown
  hair drawn back, red uniform with a white ruffled collar. Operations
  (resources): an older grey-haired man in red with rank insignia.
  Engineering: a bearded man with short dark hair, dark jacket, blue-lit
  background.

## Construction, units, resources

- **Placement**: the chosen building follows the cursor as a translucent
  green copy when the spot is valid (concrete, flat); left click orders it.
  A dark rectangular pad appears on the concrete with construction bees on
  it, then the building rises from it. Vehicles come out of the vehicle
  yard's hatch and drive to a rally point.
- **Upgrades**: hub, construction yard and vehicle yard go to stage 2/3 via
  the right arrow; the resource processor takes six ore-processor add-ons
  (round pods round the base), two at a time as hub and yard rise.
- **Mining**: mining stations on coloured deposit patches; ore is carried
  to the processor. Raw stock in a station at 46:00: 5 / 5 / 11 / 5 / 5 /
  5 (raw dilithium, talgonite, silicon, kelbonite, magnesite, dolamide); a
  processor at 34:10 held 6 / 66 / 6 / 6 / 6 / 7. The C5 targets (200
  dilithium, 1500 nitrium) took about ten minutes.
- **Power**: C1 hub generator LOW at 85% consumption, its reserve filling
  4% to 82% over 1½ minutes; C6 starts underpowered (hub consumption
  100%, demand met 31%, reserve 0%) and reaches demand met 100% with
  consumption 109-137% by 48:00; a power generator at 103:20 showed
  generator MED, consumption 50%, efficiency 120%.
- **Colony size**: 26/60 in C1, 27/50 in C8. Hub staff pool 134-146.
- **Units**: selected groups move to a clicked spot and defend themselves;
  vehicles in range attack on their own; the C8 enemy is a Klingon base
  (dark red pyramid tower, a flower-shaped building), destroyed by phaser
  fire and artillery shells.

## Map

C1-C8 look like one temperate planet: concrete pads on grass, pine woods,
mesas, a lake (C1), Jagganath by the training text. The C1 hub area reads
TERRAIN AT <373:264>/<373:275> (or 272; the digits are small). From C5
on, all readouts and exhausted mines fall in one area: rows 46-172,
columns 350-600 (C5 deposits talgonite <105:392>, <098:376>, <115:396>;
C7 silicon <061:404>, dilithium <065:396>; mines above), so C5, C7 and C8
probably share a colony site. Columns up to 595 are more than 64 blocks in
eighths, so the map isn't one of the 64x64 maps extracted (and level.md
notes the C missions have no map in the planet archives). Against every
extracted map the positions don't fit well (best: `coverd3` / `k12`, with
the two 59x columns off the map).
