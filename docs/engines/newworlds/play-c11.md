# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, bonus mission C11 (Romulan)

The bonus Romulan mission C11 ("False Sanctuary", `cvr03.stb` strings 78-87, commented out in
`level.dat`), most likely on **F1's map** (`icewrld`'s `f1`, the ice world
named Junil IV here), in a 30:52 recording (1852.1 s) with commentary (the
Drive file "Romulan Mission 15"). Times
are the recording's; messages and pages are paraphrased. Guesses are marked.
Compare the Federation and Klingon bonus missions,
[play-c9.md](play-c9.md) and [play-c10.md](play-c10.md).

How it was identified:

- **No voice clips** match (a search of the audio for every clip finds
  none), as for C9 and C10.
- **The loading box** (0:05-0:15) reads "BONUS ROMULAN MISSION" where the
  campaign missions show "ROMULAN LEVEL NN" (new: the bonus missions label
  themselves so; check whether C9 and C10 do too).
- **One page**, the INCOMING message from the imperial senate about Admiral
  Narok's seat, `cvr03.stb` string 80, in C11's part of the table (78 the
  title, 79 the stardate 9020.9 and an ice world, 81-85 the objectives, 86
  hub destroyed, 87 the failure for running out of time before the
  Federation and Klingon ships arrive). The messages name Junil IV, the
  Hubrin, the Orions, the Taubat and two ambassadors, all of which fit 79-85.
- **The map**: both mines reported exhausted, and all five mineral readings,
  lie on deposit blocks of `f1` and of no other map (below). `f1` is an
  `icewrld` map, matching the ice world of string 79 and the snow and ice
  terrain seen. So C11 is played on F1's map (fairly sure), as C10 is on F2's.

## Objectives and end

Objectives (`cvr03.stb`): 81 locate the Federation ambassador, 82 locate the
Klingon ambassador, 83 take the Federation one from the Hubrin, 84 take the
Klingon one from the Orion pirates, 85 eliminate the Orion-held Taubat
structures; 86 hub destroyed (fail); 87 time ran out before the other powers
arrived (fail). The COMMAND / MISSION OBJECTIVE panel was not seen open.

How it went: two pods searched (2:48, 11:17), the ambassador at the temple
beamed up (16:28), Narok orders an attack (16:32), then praise and a
reassignment (22:58). The Hubrin fortress is found (27:49), refuses to hand
over the Federation man (28:19), and is attacked: from 29:12 the view is a
low camera with the interface hidden except the top comm panel, Romulan craft
firing on the fortress; at about 30:20-30:40 the fortress topples. Narok's
satisfaction, then the all-objectives-complete message (21/21). **Won**, at
about 30:30. So in play the Federation ambassador dies with the fortress
instead of being taken (objective 83 met by the fortress falling, guess).

End screens: 30:40 a loading box over the three emblems; 30:45-30:52 the
Romulan emblem on black with a small button at the bottom right; the
recording ends at 30:52.
