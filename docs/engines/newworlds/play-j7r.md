# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission J7 (Romulan side)

Joint mission J7 ("The Jaws of Death", `jnt07rom.stb` string 1; the desert
planet Lupercal, `deswrld`'s `j7` map) played as the Romulans, in a
24.9-minute recording (1493.0 s) with commentary (the Drive file "Romulan
Mission 10"). Times are the recording's; messages and pages are
paraphrased. Guesses are marked. Federation side: [play-j7f.md](play-j7f.md);
Klingon side: [play-j7k.md](play-j7k.md).

Identified three ways: the loading box reads "JOINT LEVEL 07" (0:05-0:08, on
the game-start box at 4:07 and on the debriefing box at 24:45); all nine
`J7R` clips there are (`J7R005`-`J7R013`) are in the audio; and both
exhausted mines lie inside a kind-1 deposit field of `j7` (below). Campaign
place (level.dat): R1, R2, J1-J4, R8, R9, J6, **J7**, R12, J8, R14, J9 - the
tenth Romulan mission, matching the Drive file's number (the files go by
campaign place: "Mission 8" was R9, the eighth, [play-r9.md](play-r9.md);
"Mission 9" J6, [play-j6r.md](play-j6r.md)).
