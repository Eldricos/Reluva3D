# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission R2

Romulan mission R2 ("The Kingdom of the Blind", `rom02.stb`; the map `r2`,
found under `alnwrld` in the concrete CSVs; the dust world Taphet in the
Vistima system) as seen in a 43:40 recording (2620.5 s) with commentary
(Drive file "Romulan Mission 2"; YouTube title card "Romulan Mission 2
Commentary" at 0:00). This is the **second Romulan mission** in level.dat's
order (R1, R2, J1, ...). Observed behaviour; times are the recording's.
Messages are paraphrased. October 2026.

## Loading and briefing (0:05-3:40)

The loading box (0:05-0:13) names the level, "ROMULAN LEVEL 02", with the
same progress lines as the Klingon and Federation loads and the percent-done
lines,
over the three sides' emblems (Federation, Romulan, Klingon).

Then the Romulan emblem fills the screen, and the **INCOMING TRANSMISSION
page (string 3)** stands top right over it, silent, about 0:14-0:18. The
RECEIVING page (string 4) wasn't seen. So the Romulans, like the
Federation, open with the transmission page before the first clip.

## Start of play (3:42)

The game opens on the colony hub (`rch`) standing on a **concrete pad**,
four units selected (MULTISELECTION, science vehicles by icon) and the
welcome message. The compass bar at the top reads "250 N 010". A low tan
building with a slatted top stands beside the pad (`rsf`, the material
storage, selected so in C11: sure). The
NEW WORLDS panel's JUMP TO list at 4:01 shows hub, mining station, static
disruptor, photon defense and a vehicle, so the start probably has those.
The player builds the construction yard first (done 5:09).

Stock at 4:01: colony size **23/90**; dilithium 100, polycomp 988,
thermoconc 2211, transalumin 497, nitrium 3500, duranium 5000 (some may
already be spent). Later: 55/90 at 21:39, 57/90 at 22:14, 54/90 at 31:35,
58/90 at 34:50, 66/90 at about 43:00. Capacity 90 already in R2.

## Objectives and end

The COMMAND panel (4:01-4:10) shows strings 1 and 2 (title and stardate
line) and four MISSION OBJECTIVE lines, each "- INCOMPLETE": **13**
(build construction yard, vehicle yard, science station, utility
hospital), **14** (destroy all Orion units and buildings), **15** (supply
50 dilithium to the starship), **16** ([OPTIONAL] capture the Taubat
temple). **17** (rescue the Taubat prisoners) isn't listed although the
prisoners are rescued at 29:25. Each line has the Romulan emblem on a blue
square as its icon.

Won at 43:20 ("mission complete"). Order: buildings done 8:58, temple
taken 29:02, prisoners 29:25, dilithium 29:53, enemy wiped out 43:09.
Then the loading box again, with the debriefing line and ROMULAN LEVEL 02
(43:32), the Romulan emblem, and a **MISSION REPORT** box with "Mission
log." and the same four objectives, each "- COMPLETE" (43:38-43:40, end
of recording). String 11 (MISSION PASS) wasn't seen.

## Interface

- Romulan panels use the same grey-green metal frames and layout as the
  others (COMMUNICATIONS bottom centre, TRICORDER bottom right, NEW WORLDS
  top right); message text **blue** on black, section name in blue caps,
  selection details **cyan**. The Romulan emblem marks objectives.
- Briefing boxes: green heading bar, very dark red body, white text;
  NEXT and MISSION buttons bottom right.
- Portraits per section (all Romulans with the purple-black bowl cut and
  slanted brows; for matching to `ptrt/crew/lg/r_c1..`):
  - **Operations**: light tan skin, stern straight mouth, black uniform,
    a panel of small green screens behind left and right.
  - **Engineering**: darker, brown skin, narrow face, black uniform, brown
    wall behind with two pale blue-green lights at the bottom.
  - **Intelligence**: very pale, almost white skin, smiling, lavender-grey
    collar, teal lights at the top of the frame.
  - **Security**: tan skin, heavy frown, broad face, darker hair (less
    purple), black uniform, pale green-grey wall with screens.
  - **Communications**: pale skin, long thin face, blue-violet eye shadow,
    black uniform, teal panels behind (possibly a woman: unclear).
  - **Incoming message** (an Orion): blue face with a green head covering
    and green suit, red background.
