# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission J3 (Romulan side)

Joint mission J3 ("The Mask Slips", `alpwrld`'s `j3` map, the windswept
planet AP173.B in the AP173 system) played as the Romulans, in a 67.1-minute
recording (4025.6 s) with commentary (the Drive file "Romulan Mission 5").
Times are the recording's; messages are paraphrased. Other
sides: [play-j3f.md](play-j3f.md), [play-j3k.md](play-j3k.md).

Identified three ways: the loading screen (0:05-0:13) reads "JOINT LEVEL 03"
(with progress lines for the briefing, the officers, the geography, the
structures, unit manufacturing and power regulation) over the Federation, Klingon and Romulan emblems; the audio holds
eight of the nine `J3R` clips (`J3R006`-`J3R013`); and all three exhausted
mines lie on deposit blocks of `j3` (below). So the fifth Romulan mission is
J3, matching the level.dat order R1, R2, J1, J2, J3 (J1 in
[play-j1r.md](play-j1r.md), J2 in [play-j2r.md](play-j2r.md)).
