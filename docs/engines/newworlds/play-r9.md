# Star Trek: New Worlds: watched in play, mission R9

Romulan mission R9 ("I Have Become Death", `rom09.stb` string 1; the desert
world Tarikon 8 in the Tarikon system, `deswrld`'s `r9` map) in a 56.9-minute
recording (3413.2 s) with commentary (the Drive file "Romulan Mission 8").
Times are the recording's; messages and pages are
paraphrased. Guesses are marked.

Identified three ways: the loading box reads "ROMULAN LEVEL 09" (0:05-0:13,
again at 3:24-3:27 and on the debriefing box at 56:43); all ten `R9` clips
(`R9005`-`R9014`) are in the audio; and all five exhausted mines lie on
deposit blocks of `r9` (below). The Drive has no "Mission 7", so the eighth
Romulan file is R9 and **R8 is not recorded**. Campaign place (level.dat): R1,
R2, J1, J2, J3, J4, R8, **R9**, J6, ... - the eighth Romulan mission, right
after the unrecorded R8.

data sent 34:12); the study ran until 54:00 (objective 14's research); the
device reached the Klingon colony at 56:23 and went off at about 56:32 (a

percent-done line; 56:46 the Romulan emblem with CONTINUE; 56:52 a **MISSION REPORT**
panel: MISSION LOG, then objectives 12, 14 and 16, each marked COMPLETE with a
small emblem icon. The recording ends at 56:53. So **won**, three of three.

Open: only 2.5 minutes pass between the study ending (54:00) and the device
arriving at the Klingon colony (56:23) - either building it is quick and the
colonies are close, or the device was built earlier (check).
